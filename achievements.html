Verity

Chargeback defence for small online shops — HackUCD 2026

Team project · Team of four

Co-created Verity with three friends at HackUCD 2026, a one-day hackathon at UCD’s Quinn School of Business, organised by UCD Netsoc and UCD Innovation & Enterprise and sponsored by Stripe.

Our prototype tackles a challenge faced by small online shops: gathering evidence to respond to disputed card payments. Verity brings Stripe payment information, barcode checks at the packing bench, parcel photos, courier updates and customer communications into one record per order.

Records use a hash chain to make later changes detectable. AI features through OpenRouter help draft dispute responses and estimate the strength of the merchant’s case. A browser-based packing station lets staff scan product barcodes using a phone camera.

Built in a day by Aman Shorey, Joel Joseph Rajesh, Chetanjot Singh and Mirav Vaitha. Mirav led the full-stack implementation, including the order ledger, Stripe checkout and webhooks, and the packing station.

Verity is a working prototype: its demonstration shop is fictional and courier updates are simulated.

Tech Stack: Next.js · TypeScript · Stripe · PostgreSQL · Drizzle · OpenRouter
